  • cake
    •

    Ingredients Quantity
    Flour 2 cups
    sugar 1 and 1/2 cups
    Butter 1/2 cups
    Eggs 3
    Milk 1 cup
    Vainilla extract 2 teaspoons
    Baking power 2 teaspoons
    Salt 1/2 teaspoons

    Step 1: Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C). Grease and flour a cake pan.

    Step 2: In a bowl, mix the flour, baking powder, and salt. Set aside.

    Step 3: In another bowl, beat the butter and sugar until creamy. Add eggs one by one.

    Step 4: Add the vanilla extract and mix well.

    Step 5: Slowly add the dry ingredients and milk, mixing until smooth.

    Step 6: Pour the batter into the pan and bake for 40–45 minutes, or until a toothpick comes out clean.

    Step 7: Let it cool before serving or decorating.

    Recipe #2

  • Dumplings
    •

    Ingredients Quantity
    Ground pork or chicken 1 lb (450g)
    Green onions (chopped) 4
    Garlic (minced) 2 cloves
    Ginger (grated) 1 tablespoon
    Soy sauce 2 teaspoons
    Sesame oil 1 teaspoons
    Cabbage (finely chopped) 1 cup
    Dumpling wrappers 30
    Water As needed

    Step 1: In a bowl, mix ground meat, chopped cabbage, green onions, garlic, ginger, soy sauce, and sesame oil.

    Step 2: Place a small spoonful of filling in the center of each dumpling wrapper.

    Step 3: Wet the edge of the wrapper with water, fold, and press to seal.

    Step 4: Cook the dumplings by boiling, steaming, or pan-frying (your choice).

    Step 5: Serve hot with soy sauce or dipping sauce.

    Source: Just One Cookbook – Gyoza (Japanese Dumplings)

    Recipe #3

  • Sushi
    •

    Ingredients Quantity
    Sushi rice 2 cups (uncooked)
    Rice vinegar 1/3 cup
    Sugar 2 tablespoons
    Salt 1 teaspoon
    Nori (seaweed sheets) 4 sheets
    Imitation crab sticks 8 sticks
    Cucumber(sliced thin) 1 small
    Avocado (sliced) 1
    Soy sauce To taste
    Optional: Wasabi, pickled ginger To serve

    Step 1: Rinse sushi rice and cook it. Let it cool a bit, then mix with rice vinegar, sugar, and salt.

    Step 2: Place a sheet of nori on a bamboo sushi mat (shiny side down).

    Step 3: Spread a thin layer of rice evenly over the nori, leaving 1 inch at the top edge clear.

    Step 4: Add fillings (crab, cucumber, avocado) in a line across the center.

    Step 5: Roll the sushi tightly using the bamboo mat. Wet the top edge with water to seal.

    Step 6: Cut into 6–8 pieces using a wet sharp knife.

    Step 7: Serve with soy sauce, wasabi, and pickled ginger.
